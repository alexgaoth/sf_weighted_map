DataSF taxi trips (Oct–Nov 2023, Mar–Apr 2024), sampled evenly across the week

Method. SF's street network from OpenStreetMap, with every intersection and a node every ~50 m. Walking speed follows Tobler's hiking function and cycling a 150 W rider, both over the 2023 USGS lidar of the city, uphill and downhill costed separately. Signals and stop signs come from SFMTA's inventories. Car speeds come from SFCTA's INRIX data on 740 street segments, hour by hour, for the last twelve months of weekdays. Weekends come from how taxi trips slow through the week. Side streets borrow a damped share of the arterials around them. The helicopter is a what-if using the five pads the FAA lists inside the city, all of them private.

Limits. Parking isn't counted, and neither are turn restrictions. Weekend traffic is scaled from taxis citywide, not measured street by street. Treasure Island has no way in on foot or by bike: the Bay Bridge's west span has no path.

Data. Streets © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Car speeds: SFCTA, from INRIX. Taxi trips, signals, stop signs, shoreline and neighbourhoods: DataSF / SFMTA (PDDL). Elevation: USGS 3DEP. Heliports: FAA, OurAirports.
